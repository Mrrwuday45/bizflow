GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are quick answers to help you get started with BizFlow.

How does the AI Assistant work in BizFlow?
BizFlow integrates with Google Gemini AI. It analyzes your store's sales records, customer order history, and product inventory to answer natural language questions, generate business summaries, and give actionable recommendations.
Can I generate PDF invoices for my customers?
Yes! With one click during sale checkout or from the invoices tab, BizFlow automatically generates a clean, downloadable PDF invoice containing itemized prices, tax summaries, and customer details.
Is my business data private and secure?
Absolutely. Every account's data is isolated per store user in our secure database. We enforce secure password hashing, session tokens, and encrypted transport.
Can I upgrade or cancel my plan anytime?
Yes, you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your account plan at any time without hidden fees or lock-in contracts.
